Solar Products

Smart solar solutions , Everyday !

Global Suntech systems are built with proven components and architectures that suit operational goals and site conditions of every differing project.

Hybrid inverters

These coordinate solar production, battery storage and the loads within one intelligent system. Models are available with integrated MPPT charge controllers and a solar + storage management controller that enables the user to customize requirements. Battery storage grid-tied, off-grid and hybrid systems among others come in sizes from 5 kW to 150 kW. Sunsynk, Victron Energy, Deye, Invt, Solis, Growatt, ATESS IPS, Techno inverter.

PCS systems

Our bidirectional battery-based inverters are the core of energy storage and of large-scale energy storage systems. Our products cover a power range from 250 kW to 1 MW and are designed for high-demand industrial, commercial and grid-side BESS. These include all PCS including flow battery interfaces.

Batteries

Battery technologies for small business, mini-grid and utility-scale energy storage. We offer lithium iron phosphate batteries with more than 6000 cycles. They come in different configurations such as wall-mounted, rack-mounted and all-in-one systems. These include, among others, Dyness, Pylon Tech, Freedom Won, Narada, Hubble.

PV mounting and Accessories

We offer mounting structures selected to suit the intended system type. We have solutions for ground-mounted systems with optional tracking, roof-mounted systems for flat and pitched roofs, and pole-mounted structures for solar street lighting and remote systems. We also provide accessories such as cables, connectors, protection and monitoring equipment.

Architecture Selector

AC-coupled or DC-coupled?

AC-coupled systems

In an AC-coupled arrangement, solar and battery components connect on the AC side through their own conversion equipment. This approach can be considered when integrating energy storage with an existing solar system.

The best architecture depends on project goals, existing equipment and site conditions. Global Suntech provides solutions and advice regarding architectures that suit your specific needs.
