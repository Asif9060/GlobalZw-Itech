Who we are

An engineering group built for the full asset lifecycle

Global Suntech began as a solar contracting firm in 1999 and grew into a multi-discipline engineering group. Today our teams sit under one roof — electrical, civil, traffic, lighting and controls — so a client never has to reconcile four different contractors, four schedules or four sets of drawings.

From feasibility study to twenty-year O&M, we own the outcome: documented energy savings, verified uptime and infrastructure that is safe, compliant and measurable.
