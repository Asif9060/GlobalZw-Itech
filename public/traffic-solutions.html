Intelligent Traffic Design

Global Suntech creates adaptive traffic signal design based on actual needs — combining BIM, GIS and data-driven placements. We engineer intelligent layouts, planning, and timing to minimize delays, improve navigation, and optimize intersections performance.

Traffic Counting

Global Suntech uses intelligent traffic devices connected to video analytics, sensors, and connectivity tools; we also use those insights to improve safety, efficiency and city planning. We track and analyze traffic patterns using video analytics, sensors, and connectivity tools for signal timing optimization, planning, and smoother traffic flow.

/ 04

Urban Traffic Management Systems

Global Suntech delivers coordinated urban traffic control using ITS components — Traffic Management Systems (TMS), Adaptive Signal Control (ASCS), and Dynamic Message Signs (DMS) — integrated with upstream monitoring, adaptive signal control, real-time ITS features, customer needs, and real-time conditions.

Solar-Powered Traffic Solutions

Resilient traffic control, designed for solar power. Solar traffic and solar pedestrian crossing designs combine detailed traffic control with reliable, solar-ready power.
