3.1 Traffic Signals & Accessories

Traffic signals & accessories

Signal heads, traffic poles, controllers, signal cables and a full range of accessories — everything a junction needs, supplied as one package.

3.1.1 Signal Heads

High-efficiency LED signal heads in 200 mm and 300 mm sizes, available in red, amber, green, pedestrian and arrow symbols. Housings are robust, UV-stabilised polycarbonate that resists stress and harsh weather. All heads comply with SANS 1459, EN 2386, EN 60529 and EN 61000-6-x, with an operational performance of over 15 years.

  • Mounts vertically or horizontally; easy backing boards

3.1.2 Traffic Poles

Pole design is analysed with computer software across shaft, cantilever arm, base plate and anchor bolts. All materials follow international standards and project specifications; anchor bolts, nuts and washers are hot-dip galvanised. Signals mount to cantilever arms at positions set by the road layout.

Overhead Poles3.1.2

SPOH Overhead Pole

A vertical mast with a tapered cantilever arm of 6–8 m depending on road design and traffic requirements. Carries at least three signal heads and a countdown timer.

  • 6 m mast — hexagonal or round section
  • Arm tilts in the first section, runs horizontal and tapered in the second
  • Golden Yellow finish (SPOH-001) or galvanised only (SPOH-002)
Standard Poles3.1.2

Standard Pole

3,300 mm high tubular or square pipe poles — 114 mm outside diameter, 2 mm or 4.6 mm mild steel wall.

  • All cable terminations at the top via a pole-top terminal box
  • Foundation frame of 8 mm flat bars with M8 bolts and nuts
  • Square or round flange options
3.1.3 Traffic Controllers

Cutting-edge traffic signal controllers. Our flagship is the ITC 3 Global, an adaptive controller developed in partnership with SWARCO Technology, Denmark — alongside new-generation controllers that integrate traffic counting, video surveillance, red-light violation detection and data collection.

Flagship Controller3.1.3

ITC 3 Global

An adaptive, intelligent controller with built-in algorithms that interface with the world's major traffic management systems — SCATS, SCOOT, NTCIP, Omnia, SPOT/Utopia, AENOR, RSMP, STREAMS, CCOL/Ivera and more.

  • Real-time adaptive intelligence — self-learning patterns reduce congestion by over 30%
  • Smart Intersection & Smart Corridor algorithms optimise cycle time and green split
  • Built-in web interface and touch-screen; remote diagnostics and timing updates
  • Up to 24 phases per rack (48 extended); 40 loop-detector channels; 230 V AC, 50 W; 0.4 s conflict delay per SABS
Controller3.1.3

SW 200 Intelligent Traffic Controller

A 48-output intelligent networking controller with a 32-bit ARM micro-controller, 6U modular control and driving boards, in a 19-inch rack-mounted case.

  • Advanced ARM chip for powerful computing & communication
  • SCR control on outputs for higher running stability
  • Open NTCIP protocol; GPS timing for remote control and monitoring
  • Fixed time, pedestrian request, green wave, actuated and manual modes
  • Main and spare power terminals for generator connection
Controller3.1.3

Syntell Traffic Controller

Full sophistication for today's traffic engineering challenges with a fully modular, robust and simple system — comprehensive module-status feedback needs no special tools, and fault conditions are retained during outages.

  • Maximum 8 phases, 12 stages, 2 streams
  • 230 V AC supply; operating range 190–270 V AC
  • Outputs: 230 V AC, 2 A fused solid state
  • Conflict / bad-return delay: 0.4 s (SABS)
Controller3.1.3

Automotor Type UA

A robust mini controller built for the harsh southern African operating environment — modular hardware with stage-based software for a highly configurable, user-friendly system.

  • 4, 8, 12 or 16 phase configurations, with or without detection
  • Weatherproof, vandal-deterring 2 mm hot-dip galvanised cabinets; optional powder coating
  • 230 V AC supply; operating range 190–270 V AC; 0.4 s conflict delay (SABS)
